Privacy policy

This policy covers the www.asastats.com website. The ASA Stats app for iOS and Android has its own policy at https://www.asastats.com/asm-privacy/.

In short

ASA Stats collects what it needs to show you the addresses you look up and to give you an account, and nothing more. It shows no ads, contains no analytics or advertising software, does not track you across websites, and does not sell or share your data for advertising. ASA Stats never holds your keys: transactions are signed in your own wallet.

What we collect, and why

  • Without an account: the Algorand addresses and bundles you look up, so we can show them.
  • Your account: an email address and a username, and optionally your first and last name. We use the email to confirm the account and to reset your password.
  • Sign-in with Google, Apple, GitHub, Discord, Reddit or X: the identifier and the basic profile details that service shares with us, such as your email address. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address, and we keep the token Apple issues only to revoke it when you delete your account.
  • Wallet sign-in and linked addresses: the Algorand and EVM addresses you prove you own by signing a message in your wallet.
  • What you save: bundle names and their addresses, settings, alert rules, and the profit and loss settings and figures you enter.
  • Browser notifications: if you turn alerts on in a browser, the subscription your browser gives us (an address at its push service and the keys to encrypt for it), so alerts reach that browser.
  • Requests to our server: each one carries your IP address, the page asked for (which can contain an address) and your browser's name and version. We use them to answer the request, keep the service running and stop abuse.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health or financial account data.

Cookies and storage in your browser

The site sets two cookies: a session cookie that keeps you signed in (for up to four weeks) and a security cookie that protects forms against cross-site requests. It keeps your display preferences (theme, typeface, folded sections) and your wallet connections in your browser's local storage, where only your browser reads them. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. If you block cookies, you can still look up addresses but cannot sign in.

Who else processes it

  • The sign-in service you choose (Google, Apple, GitHub, Discord, Reddit, X) handles that sign-in under its own policy.
  • Your wallet (Pera, Defly and others) and the WalletConnect relay it uses see the address you connect and the messages and transactions you are asked to sign.
  • Swaps: your browser asks Folks Router, Haystack and LiquiHog for quotes and an Algorand node service (Nodely) for network data. They receive your IP address and the details of the swap you ask about.
  • Your browser's push service (Apple, Google or Mozilla) delivers alert notifications to the browsers you turned them on in.
  • Links to other sites are not ours, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

We share nothing else.

How long we keep it

We keep account data until you delete your account. We delete server logs after about two weeks and remove expired sign-in sessions every week.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile, then Account, then Delete account. Alerts stop at once, and the account and all its data are permanently deleted within 7 days. Details are on the account deletion page.

Algorand addresses and transactions are public on the blockchain and remain visible to anyone, with or without an ASA Stats account.

Security

All traffic between your browser and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never as the password itself. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We post every change on this page and, for a material change, a notice on the website. Last updated: 27 October 2026.

Contact us

Questions or requests about your data: info@asastats.com, or the ASA Stats community channels (Discord, X and Reddit).
