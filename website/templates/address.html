The label cannot go inside `.pricetip`: the currency switch and the total-without-NFTs toggle both assign to that element's `innerHTML`, so any markup within it is discarded the first time a reader flips to USD. The label is a sibling, and `.pricetip` is now a span the h1 wraps -- the data attributes move with it, since address.js reads them off `$(".pricetip")[0]`. `tabindex` and `aria-describedby`: this is the one figure on the page whose tooltip carries something available nowhere else -- the exchange rate. Every other tip repeats an amount in the other currency, which the currency switch already gives in one keystroke, so those stay pointer conveniences rather than a tab stop on each of several dozen numbers. The hidden span is what a screen reader gets, because a tip drawn as `content: attr(data-tip)` is not dependably announced; `setTip` in address.js keeps the two in step. The `.tooltip` is a *wrapper* and the focusable element is the span inside it. That is not decoration: DaisyUI reveals a tooltip on `:has(:focus-visible)` -- a focused **descendant**, not the element itself -- so a tabindex on the `.tooltip` element matches nothing and draws nothing. The wrapper is also the only safe place for it, because `setCurrency` overwrites `.pricetip`'s `innerHTML` wholesale and anything inside that would not survive. The money designs need none of this: their `.pricetip` carries no `.tooltip` class, and `.total-sub` prints the same figure and rate permanently, for everyone. {% endcomment %}
