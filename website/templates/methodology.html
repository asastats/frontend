How profit and loss is calculated

The rules behind the profit and loss widget and its PDF report

What is counted

{{ WEBSITE_NAME }} reads every transaction of your addresses from the Algorand blockchain and sorts it into swaps, liquidity added and removed, deposits into and withdrawals from dApps (lending, farms, staking), rewards, NFT purchases and sales, transfers, costs and network fees.

A transfer between the addresses of one bundle moves the units and keeps their cost basis. A transfer to any other address takes the units out at their cost; a transfer in from one brings them in at their market value on arrival.

Prices

Each event is valued at the time it happened, from the Algorand DEX pools that held the asset at that moment. ALGO is valued in US dollars through USDC. Other currencies use the European Central Bank's rate for the event's day.

Current values use today's prices, or, with the MVE valuation, what selling each holding would fetch from its pools now. ALGO and LP tokens are valued at spot either way; an LP token is valued from its pool's reserves.

An NFT without a pool is valued at its {{ WEBSITE_NAME }} appraisal, capped by the lowest price your addresses list it for. The chart over time has no NFT values and leaves them out. Professional and higher tiers can leave NFTs out of the totals altogether.

Cost basis

With average cost, units leaving an asset take the average cost of the units held at that moment. With FIFO the oldest units held leave first, with LIFO the newest, and with HIFO those that cost the most per unit. Average cost is the default; FIFO, LIFO and HIFO are available on the Cluster tier.

Realized profit or loss is what a disposal fetched minus that cost; unrealized is what the units held are worth now minus their cost. Income (rewards, airdrops, interest) is counted at its value on receipt and becomes the cost basis of those units. Costs and network fees are expenses.

Where figures are incomplete

When a sale is larger than the holding the known history shows, the part without a known cost realizes nothing; its proceeds are noted. An event without a price counts as zero and marks its asset incomplete. A holding without a current price is left out of value and unrealized. Assets that never had a market price, usually unsolicited tokens, count for nothing. An NFT without an appraisal has no current price. The widget lists the holdings without a current price, the sales beyond the known history and the events without a price under "Show the affected assets".

Your own figures

On the Asastatser tier and higher you can enter what you paid for a deposit from outside your addresses, for example an asset bought on an exchange and withdrawn. That price becomes the deposit's cost in place of its market value on arrival.

You can also enter a value per unit for a holding without a current price. It is used only while the asset has no market price, is marked "set by you", and is left out of the chart over time.

You can list your other wallets too. Units you send to one and later bring back keep the cost they left with, instead of taking their market value on return. Both transfers still count as money out and in, because the widget cannot see what the units did while away.

Your figures are kept with your account, apply to you alone, and change nothing in the classified history.

What is decoded

Besides plain payments and asset transfers, the classifier recognizes transactions with these Algorand protocols:

  • Swaps and liquidity: Tinyman (1.0, 1.1 and 2), Pact, Humble, Algofi, STAMM, Vestige, Folks Router, and Tinyman recurring orders
  • Lending and borrowing: Folks Finance (v1 and v2), Algofi, Goanna Lending
  • Liquid staking: xALGO, mALGO, Tinyman liquid staking
  • Staking and farms: Réti pooling, CompX, Pact farms, Cometa, Algofund, Gard, Gainify, Haystack, and farm and staking rewards generally
  • NFT markets: ALGOxNFT, Rand Gallery, EXA Market, Shufl, Algogems, AB2 Gallery, Downbad, Asalytic, Dartroom, THC, NFDomains; a listed NFT stays among your holdings until it sells
  • Trading and markets: Alpha Arcade, FXDX, Deridex, Lofty limit orders
  • Bridges: Wormhole, Allbridge
  • Governance: Algorand governance, Folks Finance and Tinyman governance
  • Airdrops and rewards from projects and dApps, as income

Known gaps

  • AlgoDEX order executions and some Mallow settlements are not fully decoded.
  • LP tokens of small pools without ALGO on either side may have no price.
  • An NFT swapped for another NFT has no price at the time of the swap, so the swap counts as zero.
  • Assets bought on an exchange and withdrawn to your address arrive at their market value unless you enter what you paid (see Your own figures).

This is not tax advice. Tax rules differ between countries and may require a particular cost basis method, treatment of income or of transfers; check with a tax adviser.
