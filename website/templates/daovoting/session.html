How weights are counted

A seat's weight is the smaller of its votes × 1 ASASTATS and the lowest ASASTATS it held on the seat address while voting was open{% if network != "mainnet" %} (mainnet holdings, at the same times as this session's rounds){% endif %}. Buying during voting adds nothing; selling or moving ASASTATS off the seat address lowers the weight. The weights are written after voting ends, reviewed, and then sealed.
