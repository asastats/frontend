They are one thing a reader moves between, so they get one frame. What this replaces, and why. The profile pages inherited base_home's three-column shape -- a breadcrumb rail on the left, content in the middle, actions stacked on the right. On a profile page that produced two mostly empty gutters, a ragged column of identical green pills for navigation, and page actions sitting so far from the fields they operate on that the relationship was invisible. Every control was the same weight, so nothing read as the primary one. Instead: a single centred column, a segmented sub-nav across the top, and content in cards. The sub-nav is deliberately the same control as the Sign up / Log in pair in account/base_auth.html -- one navigation idiom for the whole site rather than a second one invented here. Actions belong in the card footer beside what they act on. Blocks: profile_title the page heading profile_content cards {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
