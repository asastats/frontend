{% comment %} The appearance control, which is two different controls depending on who is looking. A signed-out reader gets a plain light/dark switch between the two brand themes and nothing else -- the full list is a signed-in feature, and offering a menu of themes to someone who cannot keep the choice would be a menu that mostly does not apply to them. A signed-in reader gets a short list, with Customize at the top leading to the appearance page where the full set of themes and the typeface pairings are chosen properly. Ten themes here, not 57: a dropdown is for choosing and the appearance page is for browsing. Which ten comes from settings.DEFAULT_THEMES_BY_SCHEME, with a Recent group above them that theme.js fills from what this browser has actually used -- so the theme you picked last is where you left it, and the ten below it never move. That is the whole reason Recent is a separate group rather than the list re-sorting itself: a menu that reorders under a reader makes their muscle memory wrong every time they use it.
