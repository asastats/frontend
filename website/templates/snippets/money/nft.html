Every fact is a `.nft-line` -- a label on the left and a figure in `--col` on the right -- so an item's estimate, its floor and what was paid for it all land in the same column as the collection subtotal above and the asset values further up the page. That is the difference from design 1's version, which lays the same facts out as three columns of running text with the numbers wherever they fall. Each line pairs a figure with what it *means*, because none of these numbers means anything alone: an estimate above a floor and one below it are the same number and opposite news. Design 1 renders "Floor price: 25.00 ALGO" and leaves the reader to compare it against a figure eight lines away.
