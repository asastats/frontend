The three sit in one grid cell rather than three columns: the thumbnails have to centre on the middle of the header itself, and a middle column only centres them when the text either side happens to be the same width. Stacked, each cell aligns to its own edge and the thumbnails to the centre of the row. The thumbnails are drawn last and lifted, so where a long collection name or a wide value reaches them they cover it rather than being squeezed -- the arrangement the old design had. The row grows to whichever cell is tallest, so a collection with sixty of them still gets the height it needs. {% endcomment %}
