{% comment %} The error pages' entire stylesheet, inlined. **These three pages link nothing and inherit nothing, deliberately.** They must render when the application is broken, which is the one situation in which `style.tw.css` may be missing, stale or unreachable. The tokens below are copied from the `asastats` and `asastats-dark` themes in `input.css`, which is the only honest way to match a stylesheet you may not load. Being a copy they can drift, so `core/tests/test_error_pages.py` compares them against the source. Which theme a reader chose is not knowable here - the choice lives in storage this page will not read - so `prefers-color-scheme` is the closest honest answer. Included rather than triplicated: if `get_template` works at all then so does an include of a sibling. {% endcomment %}
