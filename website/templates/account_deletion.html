Delete your account

On the website and in the {{ WEBSITE_NAME }} app, the same way.

How to delete it

  1. Sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then Account.
  3. Choose Delete account, enter the characters from the image and confirm.

You are signed out at once, and the account can no longer be used to sign in.

When it is deleted

The account and all its data are permanently deleted within 7 days. It cannot be restored. Alert notifications stop immediately.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, email address and name.
  • The sign-in methods connected to it: email, social accounts and wallet sign-in.
  • Your profile, settings and API token.
  • Your bundle names and linked addresses.
  • Your alerts and the browsers or devices registered to receive them.
  • Your profit and loss settings and the figures you entered.

What is not deleted

Algorand addresses and transactions are public on the blockchain. Anyone can still look them up on {{ WEBSITE_NAME }} without an account. Routine server logs are kept for a limited time as part of normal operation.

Questions

If you cannot sign in any more and want your account deleted, contact us at info@{{ WEBSITE_DOMAIN }}.
